**AnalystLab Africa Experience Lab Internship Programme**
Prepared by: Adeyemo Taiwo Aminat (Meenah)

business intelligence: understanding the business problem, profiling and cleaning the data,
querying it in SQL, exploring it in Python, and building a Power BI dashboard.

## Week 2 Key Findings
- Mobile App carries 42.5% of all transaction volume but has one of the higher failure/reversal
  rates (8.64%), making it the top priority for reliability work.
- International transactions are flagged for risk review at roughly twice the rate of domestic
  ones (36.9% vs 18.9%), across every transaction type.
- A small share of transactions (6.1%, all over NGN 200k) drives 42.4% of total transaction value.
- Overall transaction success rate is 90.47%, with room to reduce the 9.5% Failed/Reversed/Pending share.
- Customer segments behave more similarly than differently across transaction frequency and
  account health, suggesting platform-wide fixes will help broadly rather than needing
  segment-specific strategies.
